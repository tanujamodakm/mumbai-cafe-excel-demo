# Mumbai Cafe Business Intelligence Dashboard

An Excel-based Business Intelligence project built using Mumbai cafe data. The project focuses on data preparation, feature engineering, pivot table analysis, dashboard design, and business storytelling to explore cafe performance across different areas of Mumbai.

## Dataset

The original dataset contains information about Mumbai cafes, including:

* Cafe Name
* Area
* Rating
* Total Ratings
* Address
* Latitude
* Longitude

Dataset Source:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/superveer70/mumbai-cafes-datasets/data

## Methodology

To enhance the analytical value of the dataset, several derived metrics were created using Excel formulas and business assumptions.

### Popularity Score

Measures overall cafe popularity by combining customer ratings and review volume.

**Formula**

```text
Popularity Score = Rating × Total Ratings
```

### Estimated Monthly Visitors

Approximates customer footfall using review activity.

**Formula**

```text
Estimated Monthly Visitors = Total Ratings × 25
```

### Average Spend

Assigned as a synthetic metric based on cafe positioning and category.

### Estimated Revenue

Estimates potential monthly revenue generated by each cafe.

**Formula**

```text
Estimated Revenue = Estimated Monthly Visitors × Average Spend
```

### Cafe Category

Cafes were classified into:

* Premium
* Popular
* Average

  * Total Reviews

* Average Rating by Area

* Revenue Analysis by Area

* Top 10 Cafes by Popularity Score

* Cafe Category Distribution

* Popularity vs Customer Satisfaction Scatter Plot

* Interactive Slicers for Area and Cafe Category

## Key Insights

* Cafe ratings across Mumbai are relatively consistent, ranging from approximately 4.1 to 4.5.
* Certain areas contribute significantly more estimated revenue than others.
* Popular cafes account for the largest share of the cafe ecosystem.
* Review volume alone does not strongly predict customer ratings, as shown by the weak correlation in the scatter plot.

## Note

Popularity Score, Estimated Monthly Visitors, Average Spend, Estimated Revenue, and Cafe Category are engineered metrics created for analytical purposes. These values are synthetic estimates and do not represent actual business figures.
